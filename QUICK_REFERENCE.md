# 🎯 Quick Reference - New Features

## 📊 Price History Charts

**What it does:** Shows visual price trends over time

**Commands:**
- `/chart` - Select product from list
- `/chart B0ABCD1234` - View specific product

**What you see:**
- 📈 Price trend line
- 💰 Average price (dashed line)
- 🎯 Your target price (if set)
- 📊 Statistics: Current, Average, Low, High
- 📅 Tracking duration

**Use it when:**
- Deciding if current price is good
- Checking price volatility
- Seeing long-term trends
- Planning when to buy

---

## ⚡ Flash Deals

**What it does:** Alerts you to massive price drops (>20% in 24h)

**Commands:**
- `/flashdeals` - View all active flash deals

**Features:**
- 🔥 Only shows deals >20% off
- 💰 Shows exact savings amount
- 🎯 Filters by your target price
- ⚡ Instant notifications
- 🛒 Quick "Buy Now" button

**Smart filtering:**
- Minimum £10 price (avoids spam on cheap items)
- Only alerts if below your target price
- 6-hour cooldown per product
- Automatic detection every 30 mins

**Use it when:**
- Looking for urgent deals
- Checking if prices crashed
- Planning impulse buys
- Maximizing savings

- 📋 Recent 5 savings

- 📈 Statistics

**Automatic tracking:**
- No setup needed
- Runs in background
- Updates instantly
- Historical data saved

**Use it when:**
- Feeling accomplished! 🎉
- Justifying bot usage
- Comparing deals
- Motivating yourself

## ⭐ Product Ratings

**What it does:** Shows Amazon ratings in your product lists

**No commands needed** - Automatic!

- 🎯 Quality emoji indicators

**Emoji meanings:**
- 🌟 Excellent (4.5+)

**Where you see it:**
- Product lists (`/list`)

- Deal notifications

**Use it when:**
- Comparing products
- Deciding quality vs. price
- Avoiding bad products
- Finding best value

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

**Chart not generating?**
- Wait a few seconds (external API)
- Check product has 2+ price points
- Try again in 30 seconds

**No flash deals?**
- None active right now! ⏳
- Check back after next price check (30 mins)
- Add more products to track

**Savings showing £0?**
- Start tracking products
- Wait for price drops
- Savings accumulate over time

**Ratings missing?**
- Updates weekly (automatic)
- Amazon blocked temporarily
- Try refreshing in 1-2 days
